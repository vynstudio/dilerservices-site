Before You Order

Diler Services requires the proposed company name, the names and addresses of the members or managers, a mailing address, and a contact email. A checklist is provided with each order.

LLC formation is a $89 service fee plus the $125 Florida state fee, passed through at cost. The formation bundle with EIN filing totals $299.

If Diler Services does not submit the filing within 1 business day of receiving complete information, the $89 formation service fee is refunded. State fees are not refundable once filed.
