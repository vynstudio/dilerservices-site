Forming an LLC

A limited liability company (LLC) is a business entity created by filing Articles of Organization with the state. Owners should consult an attorney or CPA about liability and tax treatment for their situation.

Yes. Diler Services forms Florida LLCs for business owners in every state. Each LLC must maintain a Florida registered agent, which Diler Services provides.

Diler Services submits the filing within 1 business day of receiving complete information. The State of Florida processes filings in the order received and sends a confirmation once the filing is approved.

LLC formation includes preparation of the Articles of Organization, filing with the State of Florida, payment of the state fee at cost, and delivery of the state confirmation.

Diler Services requires the proposed company name, the names and addresses of the members or managers, a mailing address, and a contact email. A checklist is provided with each order.

Registered Agent

A registered agent receives legal process, notices and demands on behalf of the company and forwards them to the company. Florida law requires every LLC to maintain a registered agent and registered office in Florida.

Under section 605.0113 of the Florida Statutes, the agent must be a Florida resident or an authorized business entity whose business address is the registered office address in Florida.

Yes. Registered agent clients receive same-day forwarding of documents received on behalf of their company.

Registered agent service is $13.99 per month or $169 per year.

EIN and Compliance

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is a federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The IRS issues EINs free of charge. Diler Services charges a $95 service fee to prepare and submit the application.

The IRS advises forming the entity with the state before applying for an EIN. Diler Services files the EIN application after the state approves the LLC.

Every Florida LLC must file an annual report each year between January 1 and May 1. The State of Florida assesses a $400 late fee on LLC reports filed after May 1.

The State of Florida administratively dissolves entities that have not filed by the third Friday of September. A dissolved company may apply for reinstatement. Diler Services prepares reinstatements for $299 plus the state fees at cost.

Pricing and Refunds

LLC formation is a $89 service fee plus the $125 Florida state fee, passed through at cost. The formation bundle with EIN filing totals $299.

Annual report $99 ($149 if filed late), DBA $129, amendments $99 and reinstatement $299, each plus the state fee at cost.

If Diler Services does not submit the filing within 1 business day of receiving complete information, the $89 formation service fee is refunded. State fees are not refundable once filed.

If the State of Florida rejects the requested name, Diler Services refiles with a new name at no additional service fee.
