How to register an LLC in Florida

  1. Choose a name. It must be available in Florida and end in "LLC", "L.L.C." or "Limited Liability Company".
  2. Appoint a registered agent. Every Florida LLC needs one with a street address in the state. See our Florida registered agent service.
  3. File Articles of Organization. The filing goes to the Florida Division of Corporations with the $125 state fee. We prepare and submit it within 1 business day of complete information.
  4. Get an EIN. Once the state approves your LLC, apply for an EIN with the IRS. Learn how to get an EIN for your LLC.

Staying compliant after you file

Every Florida LLC files an annual report between January 1 and May 1, and the state adds a $400 late fee after May 1. Our annual report service is $99, and registered agent clients get a reminder before each deadline.
