Last updated October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Diler Services LLC ("Diler Services") collects, uses and protects information provided through dilerservices.com.

Information collected

Diler Services collects the information a visitor submits through the site forms, such as name, email address, business name, the requested service and message content. To complete a filing, Diler Services also collects the information the State of Florida or the IRS requires.

How information is used

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare requested filings.
  • To submit filings to government agencies on the client's behalf.
  • To provide registered agent service and forward documents.
  • To send service-related reminders, such as annual report deadlines.

Public records

Information included in a state filing, such as names and addresses on Articles of Organization or an annual report, becomes part of the public record maintained by the State of Florida.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form processor. Diler Services does not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The site does not set analytics cookies unless the visitor accepts them through the cookie banner. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics is loaded only after consent.

Data security and retention

Diler Services uses reasonable measures to protect information and retains it only as long as needed to provide services and meet record-keeping obligations.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@dilerservices.com.
