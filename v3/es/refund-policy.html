Política de reembolsos

Last updated October 20, 2026

1-business-day submission guarantee

Diler Services submits each LLC formation filing within 1 business day of receiving complete information. If Diler Services does not submit within that time, the $89 formation service fee is refunded in full.

State fees

State fees are paid to the State of Florida. Once a filing has been submitted, state fees are not refundable.

Free name refile

If the State of Florida rejects the requested business name, Diler Services refiles with a new name chosen by the client at no additional service fee.

How to request a refund

Refund requests may be sent to info@dilerservices.com with the order details.
