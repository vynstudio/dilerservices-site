Forming an LLC

A limited liability company (LLC) is a business entity created by filing Articles of Organization with the state. Ask an attorney or CPA about liability and tax treatment for your situation.

Registered agent

A registered agent receives legal process, notices and demands for the company and forwards them. Florida law requires every LLC to keep a registered agent and registered office in Florida.

Under section 605.0113 of the Florida Statutes, the agent must be a Florida resident or an authorized business entity whose business address is the registered office in Florida.

Yes. Registered agent clients receive same-day forwarding of documents received for their company.

$13.99 per month or $169 per year.

EIN and compliance

An Employer Identification Number is a federal tax number issued by the IRS free of charge. Our $95 fee covers preparing and submitting the application.

The IRS advises forming the entity with the state first. We file the EIN application after the state approves the LLC.

Every Florida LLC files an annual report between January 1 and May 1. The state adds a $400 late fee to LLC reports filed after May 1.

Florida administratively dissolves companies that have not filed by the third Friday of September. A dissolved company can apply for reinstatement, which we prepare for $299 plus the state fees at cost.
