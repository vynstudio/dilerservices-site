What a Florida registered agent does

A registered agent receives legal process, notices and demands for your company and passes them on. Florida law requires every LLC to keep a registered agent and a registered office at a street address in Florida, under section 605.0113 of the Florida Statutes.

Why businesses choose a professional registered agent

  • Our Florida street address serves as your registered office on the public record.
  • Documents received for your company are forwarded the same day.
  • You get a reminder before each annual report deadline.
  • Owners who live outside Florida get a Florida address that meets the requirement.

Switching your registered agent

A Florida LLC can change its registered agent by filing with the Division of Corporations. Contact us and we will explain the steps for your company. Forming a new company? Add registered agent service when you register your LLC in Florida.
