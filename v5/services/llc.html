Questions on a Florida LLC

How do I form a Florida LLC?

Share the business name, the address, and who is involved. We prepare the articles and file them with the State of Florida.

What does the $89 cover?

It is our fee to prepare and file the Florida LLC. Florida's state fee is $125, listed on its own.

What if Florida rejects the filing?

If the state rejects your filing and we can't fix it, we refund our fee.
