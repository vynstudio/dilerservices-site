Reviews

Questions

Straight answers.

What does Diler Services handle?

Diler Services prepares and files LLC and corporation formation, requests an EIN, provides Florida registered agent service, files annual reports, and keeps documents and deadlines in one workspace.

How do I register an LLC?

Share the company name, the owners, and the state. We prepare the articles and file them. You can follow the order from Filed, to Approved, to EIN issued.

How do I get an EIN for an LLC?

The company is formed with the state first. We then request the EIN, and the IRS letter is saved in your document vault.

What does a Florida registered agent do?

The agent provides a Florida address for the public record, receives official notices and service of process, and forwards that mail to you.

Can I keep my home address off the public record?

Yes. A registered agent address can be the address on the record. Official mail goes there and is forwarded to you.

What if the state rejects the filing?

If the state rejects your filing and we can't fix it, we refund our fee.

Is Diler Services a law firm?

No. Diler Services is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Which languages do you support?

English and Spanish.
