Questions

Questions on your Florida LLC.

What does Diler Services handle?

We file Florida LLCs and Florida corporations, request an EIN for your LLC, serve as your Florida registered agent, file your Florida annual report, and prepare a Florida LLC operating agreement. Your documents and deadlines stay in one place.

How do I form a Florida LLC?

Tell us your business name, your address, and who's involved. We prepare the paperwork and file it with the State of Florida. You can follow the order from Filed, to Approved, to EIN issued.

How do I get an EIN for my LLC?

We request your EIN for you, so you can open a bank account and file taxes. The IRS letter is saved in your document vault.

What does a Florida registered agent do?

Every Florida LLC needs a registered agent. We provide a Florida address, accept official mail, and let you know. You use our address, not your home address.

Can I keep my home address off the public record?

Yes. The registered agent address can be the address on the record. Official mail comes to us, and we forward it to you.

When is the Florida annual report due?

Florida requires every LLC to file a report each year. It's due between January 1 and May 1. We remind you and file your Florida annual report for you.

Do I need a Florida LLC operating agreement?

Florida doesn't make you file one. You still want one. It sets who owns what, who makes decisions, and what happens if someone leaves. We give you a Florida LLC operating agreement that fits how you run things.

What if Florida rejects the filing?

If Florida rejects your filing and we can't fix it, we refund our fee.

Is Diler Services a law firm?

No. Diler Services is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Which languages do you support?

English, Spanish, and Portuguese.
