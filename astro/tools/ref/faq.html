FAQ

Frequently asked questions

  • Are you a law firm?
    No. Diler Services is a fictitious name of Diler Dynamics Group LLC. We are not a law firm and we do not give legal or tax advice. We prepare and file documents as you direct.
  • Why are there two prices on every package?
    The first number is our service fee, the only amount we earn. The second is the Florida state fee, which we pass through at cost on a separate line.
  • What does the yearly registered agent plan include?
    Registered Agent is $17.99 per month or $169 per year. We serve as your Florida registered agent and registered office, and log and forward mail the same business day. It includes 5 mail scans a year and annual report reminders at 90, 30, and 7 days.
  • What is Compliance Plus?
    Compliance Plus is $229 per year. It includes the registered agent service, and we file the annual report each year when you confirm your information. The state fee for that report, $138.75 for an LLC, is billed when it is filed.
  • Can you get an EIN?
    Yes. LLC Standard and LLC Premium include an EIN from the IRS. The IRS does not charge a fee for an EIN. Please do not send a Social Security number or ITIN through the website form.
  • What languages do you work in?
    English, Spanish, and Portuguese.
  • Can I keep my home address off the public record?
    Yes. The registered agent address can be the address on the record. Official mail comes to us, and we forward it to you.
  • When is the Florida annual report due?
    Florida requires every LLC to file a report each year. It's due between January 1 and May 1. We remind you and can file it for you.
  • Do I need a Florida LLC operating agreement?
    Florida doesn't make you file one. You still want one. It sets who owns what, who makes decisions, and what happens if someone leaves.
