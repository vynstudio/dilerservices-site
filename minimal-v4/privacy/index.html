Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated October 20, 2026.

This policy describes how Diler Services LLC collects, uses, and protects information you provide while asking about a filing or hiring the firm to prepare one.

Information collected

We collect what you send: your name, email address, business name, the service you ask for, and your message. Completing a filing also requires the information the State of Florida or the IRS asks for.

How information is used

  • To answer you and prepare the filing you requested.
  • To submit that filing to the government agency.
  • To provide registered agent service and forward documents.
  • To send service reminders, including annual report deadlines.

Public records

Names and addresses that appear on a state filing become part of the public record kept by the State of Florida.

Messages

The contact form opens a message in your email application, addressed to info@dilerservices.com. Diler Services does not sell personal information.

Cookies

This site does not set analytics cookies.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service and meet record-keeping duties.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to info@dilerservices.com.
