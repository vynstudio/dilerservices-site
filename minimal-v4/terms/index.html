These terms govern use of this site and the services of Diler Services LLC.

Nature of the service

Diler Services prepares and submits business filings from the information you provide, and serves as registered agent when hired to do so. Diler Services LLC is not a law firm and does not give legal or tax advice. Those questions belong with a licensed attorney or CPA.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of what you send. We file that information as you provide it.

Fees

Service fees are the prices published on this site. State fees are passed through at cost and are paid to the State of Florida. Registered agent service is billed separately from formation and from the bundle.

Timing

We submit filings within one business day of receiving complete information. Approval times are set by the State of Florida and the IRS.

Refunds

If we do not submit an LLC formation within one business day of complete information, the $89 formation service fee is refunded in full. State fees are paid to the State of Florida and are not refundable once a filing has been submitted. If the state rejects the requested business name, we refile with a new name you choose at no additional service fee. Refund requests go to info@dilerservices.com with the order details.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, liability for a claim about a service is limited to the service fee paid for that service.
